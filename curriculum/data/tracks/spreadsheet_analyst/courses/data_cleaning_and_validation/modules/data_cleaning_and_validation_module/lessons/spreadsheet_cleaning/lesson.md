# Text, Dates, Numbers & Duplicates

Preserve raw data while creating clean, analysis-ready text, phone, and date fields.

## What you will learn

- Keep source fields intact and create separate clean fields.
- Use text functions to remove extra spaces and standardize capitalization.
- Convert inconsistent phone and date text into consistent analytical values.

## Learning sequence

1. **Learn & Predict** — understand the theory and study a separate worked example.
2. **Try It** — use the Google Sheets feature with guided strategy, without being given the finished formula.
3. **Challenge** — solve an independent Northstar Operations business task.
4. **Transfer** — apply the underlying principle to a new scenario.

## Key takeaways

- Raw source values should remain available for audit and troubleshooting.
- TRIM removes unwanted spaces and PROPER standardizes capitalization.
- SUBSTITUTE and DATEVALUE can transform inconsistent text into usable fields.
