# Review and approve project brief

**Project:** Retail Operations Performance Dashboard  
**Stage:** Discovery  
**Estimated focused time:** about 45 minutes

## What you're doing

Review the project plan and make sure the problem, audience, questions, scope, deliverables, and success criteria all fit together.

Use the Studio or Notebook tab in this milestone when one is available. Open an external tool only when the work genuinely belongs there, such as Power BI or a spreadsheet editor.

## Why it matters

A clear brief gives the whole project one shared direction.

## Before you start

- Read the current project overview and any supplied brief.
- Have the target audience and the main business problem in mind.

## Steps

1. Open the Project Brief Studio.
2. Check who the project is for and what decision the analysis should support.
3. Make the business questions specific enough to answer with data.
4. Set the scope, deliverables, and success criteria.
5. Fix anything that conflicts or is still vague.
6. Save the approved brief.

## You're done when

- [ ] The approved brief clearly explains who the project is for, what decision it supports, what it will answer, and what success looks like.
- [ ] Someone new to the project can understand the problem, audience, questions, scope, and expected result.
- [ ] The brief does not promise work or data that the project will not include.
- [ ] You saved the real project artifact, not only a note inside Career Accelerator.
- [ ] Any assumptions, exceptions, and unresolved questions are easy to find.

## What to save

- `documentation/project_brief.md`
- `PROJECT_CHARTER.md`

Keep only the files that help another analyst understand or reproduce the work.

## Keep in mind

- Do not turn the brief into a long report.
- Do not list broad topics as business questions.
- Mark assumptions instead of presenting them as facts.

## Next step

Use the approved brief to finalize the data source and specification.
