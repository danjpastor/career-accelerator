# Spreadsheet Academy learning design

Spreadsheet Academy uses one continuing Northstar Operations Google Sheet and an original four-step lesson rhythm:

1. **Learn & Predict** — a short explanation of why the skill matters, plain-language theory, and a worked example that uses data separate from the learner's task.
2. **Try It** — guided Google Sheets practice. The lesson explains the interface and the strategy but does not provide the finished task formula.
3. **Challenge** — an independent business task in the Northstar Operations workbook.
4. **Transfer** — a new scenario that tests whether the learner understands the principle rather than memorizing cell addresses.

## Answer policy

- Workbook prompts name the skill, business result, sheet, and required output.
- Prompts do not contain completed formulas.
- Worked examples may show generic syntax using unrelated data.
- Hints progress from concept reminder to location and strategy guidance.
- Exact formulas and final answers appear only under **View Solution**.
- **Check My Work** gives specific feedback about the missing or incorrect workbook requirement.

## Google Sheets workflow

The learner links one shared Google Sheet once. Career Accelerator opens that sheet for each task and downloads the latest `.xlsx` export when the learner selects **Check My Work**. Google Sheets is the teaching source of truth.
