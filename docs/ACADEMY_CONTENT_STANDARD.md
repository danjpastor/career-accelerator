# Academy Content Standard

Academy lessons are written for a complete beginner.

- Use short, natural sentences and familiar workplace examples.
- Define a term before expecting the learner to use it.
- Explain why a skill matters before giving an activity.
- Keep worked examples separate from graded exercises.
- State the business outcome and required skill without providing the finished answer.
- Explain mistakes specifically and supportively.
- Never require outside documentation to understand the lesson.
- Keep all wording, datasets, examples, scenarios, and solutions original to Career Accelerator.
